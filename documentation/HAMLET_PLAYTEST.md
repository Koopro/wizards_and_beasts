# Hamlet playtest: a checklist for players

You are the first person to play a wizarding hamlet. Play it the way you would play any Minecraft world. You don't need
commands, cheats or debug screens. For each step, note what happened and how it felt; the questions under each step help.

Write down anything that confused you, annoyed you or felt good. "I didn't know where to go" counts as much as a crash.

## Before you start (for whoever hosts the world)

- A new world, made with this build. A hamlet that existed before this build may have a flooded cellar (see the end of this
  page); please don't use an old world for this test.
- Survival mode and Easy difficulty, unless you are testing something else.
- If two of you play, both join the same server. Steps 5, 6 and 10 are worth repeating together.

## The steps

**1. Find a hamlet with the Wayfarer's Chart.**
The chart turns up in your inventory once you have finished the Apprentice's Path, the short list of first steps the game
gives you (choose a heritage, get a wand, cast, learn and practise a spell, study a creature). Open the chart like a map
and follow it.
- How long did the Path take? Did you know what to do next at each step?
- When the chart arrived, did you notice it? Was it clear where it pointed?

**2. Walk up to the plaza.**
- What did you see first from a distance? Did the place look like somewhere people live?
- Could you get in on foot from the side you came from?

**3. Look around every building.**
There are five: the Keeper's house, the wandmaker, the apothecary, the Reading Room and the goblins' bank counter.
- Could you tell which building was which from outside? From inside?
- Did any door not open, or close on you, or did someone stand in the way? Write down which door.

**4. Talk to all five inhabitants.**
Right-click a person to speak. Their board lists things to ask about.
- Did everyone answer? Did anything they said sound wrong, repeated or odd?

**5. Buy something from the wandmaker.**
Shops take the money from your Gringotts vault, not from your pocket.
- Was the price clear? Did the money leave your vault and the item reach your inventory?
- If a friend has the same board open, does their stock count go down when you buy?

**6. Buy something from the apothecary.**
- Did you find what is for sale without scrolling a long way?
- Try buying something you can't afford. Did the game tell you why?

**7. Visit the Reading Room.**
- Was it clear what the room is for?

**8. Take a lesson.**
Ask the scholar for a lesson on their board.
- Did you learn something? Did the game show you what you learned?

**9. Find the goblin teller.**
The bank counter is the building with the sandstone front and iron doors. The iron doors at the sides are meant to stay
shut. Use the middle one.

**10. Deposit and withdraw pocket coins.**
Click the arrows to put Sickles and Knuts in and take them out. Try clicking quickly several times.
- Did every click count? If the game refused a click, did it tell you so?
- Count your coins before and after: did anything go missing or appear from nowhere?

**11. Take a contract from the Keeper.**
- Was it clear what the contract asks and what it pays?

**12. Find the Undercroft and go down.**
It is under one of the buildings. Look for a trapdoor in the floor with a ladder below it.
- Did you find it on your own? How long did it take? (If you gave up, note that and ask for a hint.)

**13. Use the knocker.**
Downstairs is a wall that looks like ordinary brickwork, with a small dark button set in the bricks above it. Press the
button.
- Did the wall open? Could you walk through?
- Would you have spotted the button without being told it was there?

**14. Strike an inhabitant and see them come back.**
Hit one of the inhabitants until they vanish. They don't die; they Apparate away with a crack and come back at the next
dawn. Then sleep in a bed through the night (everyone on the server has to sleep), or just wait.
- Did they vanish without dropping anything?
- Were they back, with the same name, when you woke up? If not, how long did it take?
- Is their shop still the same (the same stock, the same prices)?

**15. Come back after a restart.**
Quit (or stop the server) and come back.
- Are the same five people there, by name?
- Is the Undercroft wall still open? Does your contract still show? Is your vault balance what it was?

## At the end

- What was the best moment? What was the worst?
- What would you have liked to do in the hamlet that you couldn't?
- Did anything look broken: floating blocks, holes, water where it shouldn't be, someone stuck in a wall?

## Things we already know about

- If someone is struck just before dawn, they stay away about two more minutes.
- The bank's two side doors are iron and don't open by hand.
- Hamlets in worlds made before this build can have a flooded cellar under the apothecary. New worlds don't.
- Some shop names are borrowed from famous wizarding shops. That's a placeholder.
