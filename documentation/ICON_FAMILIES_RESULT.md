# Icon Families Result — 2026-11-03

Visual Renaissance Batch 2 on `feat/visual-renaissance`: mob-effect icons, ability icons, hair items,
Death Eater masks, and icons whose generator is missing or retired. Art only: no identifier,
registration, duration or behaviour changed. The one data change is the `icon` path in 16 ability
JSONs, which only the wheel reads.

The direction was approved on a look sheet (6 masks, 5 hair, 4 effects, 4 abilities, old above new)
before the rest was drawn.

## Redesigned

- **Masks (6):** carved bone, the worn palette, one sculpt; each casting's feature pushed to read at
  16 px — plain, tear runnels (Classic), scaled ridge + coil (Serpentine), torn sockets + open mouth
  (Screaming), horns (Horned), crack + chipped cheek (Cracked). Horned and Serpentine change the
  silhouette, not just the face.
- **Hair (5 + 5 fade frames):** one cut per creature, same engine: unicorn tied tail lock (brightest
  white, silver band), veela plait with gold ties, demiguise silk fan (the 5 fade frames follow),
  thestral ring-bound switch with slate strands, troll whiskers on granite hide. Strands are split by
  the outline and wave out of phase — solid locks read as socks or feathers, strands in step as a comb.
- **Mob effects (18):** redrawn — basilisk_venom (the fang and a bead of venom), crucio_sanity_drain
  (head split by a red crack), engorgio / reducio (one pair: arrows flying out in gold, driving in in
  blue), expelliarmus_disarmed, jelly_legs, langlock (lips under a padlock), muffliato (an ear full of
  buzzing), obscuro (blindfold over an eye), protego_shield (pale ward with a gem of light), sectumsempra
  (cuts and drops), mandrake_restoration (the mandrake). New — camouflage (a figure half dissolved),
  floo_protected (emerald flames on a grate), infatuation (heart struck through), living_death (coffin
  with asphodel), scent_tracking (paw print), wolfsbane (aconite).
- **Abilities (16):** `tools/ability_icons.py`. The spell menu's language (kind-metal rim, semantic
  glyph colour, lit top-left, keylined), with an octagon frame so an innate power never reads as a
  spell, and the dark field because the wheel sits on the NIGHT surface. Kinds → metals: offensive gilt,
  defensive steel, utility silver, dark black iron, heritage bronze, transformation verdigris, legendary
  white gold (reserved). Obscurus Grasp/Surge reuse their spell glyphs.
- **Broom fittings + courier parcel (7):** claimed from the retired `item_icons.py` (soft, unoutlined):
  streamlined twigs, balanced tail, reinforced binding, anti-jinx varnish, braking charm, cushioning
  charm, courier parcel.

## Validation

- Pixels: every sandbox sprite rendered and diffed with the live tree before writing — exactly the 23
  item and 18 effect icons intended differed; the hank trio and all other sprites byte-identical.
- `art_sync_check --only item_sprites,effect_sprites,effect_icons,ability_icons,spell_glyphs`: 5/5 in sync.
- Files: effect icons 18x18, item icons 16x16, ability icons 16x16, hard alpha (the demiguise fade
  frames are deliberately translucent), resource paths resolved through the item definitions.
- Tests: `EffectAndAbilityIconAssetTest` (new: every registered effect has an icon; every non-debug
  ability names an existing 16 px hard-alpha icon) — mutation-checked, both fail with an icon removed.
  `VisualStyleConformanceTest`, `SpellIconAssetTest`, ability tests green. Full `./gradlew test`:
  2,997 tests, 6 failing — the known wand-rig baseline (`WandAppearanceTest`, `WandModelContractTest`,
  `WandLookAssetTest`), unchanged by this work.

### In-client capture

A real client (`UiScreenCapture`, 1920x1080, GUI scales 2 and 4) with four new dev-only steps:

| Step | Shows |
|---|---|
| `inventory_icons` | the survival inventory holding every changed item, the unchanged hank trio, the feathers, the broom kit, and vanilla string/feather/leather for scale |
| `effects_<n>` / `effects_hud_<n>` | every mod effect, five at a time, in the inventory's effect panel and in the HUD status row |
| `ability_wheel_all` | the ability wheel with every wheel-eligible ability |

Seen: the masks read as six different castings in a slot; every hair cut reads as hair beside vanilla
string and feather; the effects read in the HUD at scale 2; the wheel shows all 16 icons in their night
sockets, the two debug abilities on their monogram. No missing textures in the log.
`courier_parcel` is not in the inventory shot: an unaddressed parcel deletes itself after a second
(`CourierParcelItem.inventoryTick`), so it was judged on the sheet and in JEI only.

Run it: `WB_UI_CAPTURE=<dir> WB_UI_CAPTURE_ONLY=inventory_icons,ability_wheel_all,effects_0,effects_hud_0,...`
on a disposable save copy. If the worktree is shared, start the client from a frozen copy of
`build/classes` + `build/resources`: a parallel `processResources` once gutted the datapack load mid-run.
